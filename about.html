Rooted in the community

We believe good farming can change a table — and a future.

Likho Agriculture began with a simple idea: grow food that tastes good, does good, and brings people closer to the land. Today, we work with the seasons and with our neighbours to cultivate fresh produce using practical, responsible methods.

We are always learning — from the soil, from the weather, and from the people we serve. Every harvest is a chance to do things a little better.
